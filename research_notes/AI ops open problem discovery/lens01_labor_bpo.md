## Key Question 1 — Which back-office and front-office roles have the worst hiring difficulty or turnover in 2025-2026?

### Takeaway
Hard data (MGMA, ADA HPI, insurance agency studies) shows the worst-to-hire roles are clinical (medical assistants, dental hygienists and assistants). Front-office and admin roles are the *turnover* hotspot, and are a steady, moderately hard hire. Billers and coders are much less often named "hardest to hire" in 2025 (10% and 9%) than the common story suggests. At independent insurance agencies the pain is burnout and an aging workforce more than raw vacancy numbers.

### Cited Findings
**Medical practices (MGMA)**
- MGMA Stat poll, 6 May 2025, 420 responses. Hardest staff role to hire: medical assistants 47%, nurses 15%, billers 10%, coders 9%, other 18% — [MGMA](https://www.mgma.com/mgma-stat/why-medical-assistants-are-still-tougher-to-hire-today-than-nurses-coders-and-other-medical-practice-staff); see also [MGMA](https://www.mgma.com/mgma-stat/medical-assistants-tackling-practices-worst-hiring-problem)
- MGMA 2025 turnover poll, 357 responses. 35% said turnover was the same as last year, 35% lower, 29% higher. Practices with rising turnover named MAs and **front-office staff** as the hotspots — [MGMA](https://www.mgma.com/mgma-stat/can-staff-turnover-continue-to-be-tamed-in-medical-practices-into-2026)
- Earlier (March **2023**) MGMA poll: medical coders were the hardest revenue-cycle role to hire, at 34% — [MGMA data story](https://mgma.com/data/data-stories/bottom-line-impacts-from-revenue-cycle-staffing-ch)
- A June 2025 commentary lists 2025 practice challenges as prior authorizations, a "staffing squeeze", and rising costs — [Physicians Practice](https://www.physicianspractice.com/view/2025-s-medical-practice-challenges-prior-authorizations-a-staffing-squeeze-and-rising-costs)

**Dental practices (ADA Health Policy Institute, via ADA News and Becker's)**
- In ADA HPI's Q3 2025 polling, hygienists and assistants are still the hardest roles to recruit. 26.8% of dentists had recruited administrative staff in the prior three months — [Becker's Dental](https://www.beckersdental.com/staffing-issues/how-dentists-are-faring-against-workforce-shortages/)
- In one January poll (the year is not confirmed in the snippet), 44.9% of owner dentists called recruiting administrative staff "extremely challenging", against 75.7% for hygienists — [Becker's Dental](https://www.beckersdental.com/staffing-issues/these-positions-are-the-most-challenging-to-recruit-now-say-owner-dentists/)
- About 62% of new dentists named staffing shortages as their top expected challenge for 2025 — [ADA News, Mar 2025](https://adanews.ada.org/new-dentist/2025/march/staffing-shortages-top-expected-challenges-this-year)
- "55.3% of 751 responding dentists named insurance issues among their top three challenges for 2026." This appeared in a search summary next to dental-verification vendor pages. **The page it came from is uncertain, so verify it with ADA HPI before use** — possibly [Stealth Agents research page (secondary)](https://stealthagents.com/research/dental-insurance-verification-workload-statistics-2026)

**Independent insurance agencies**
- The 2025 Liberty Mutual/Safeco Independent Agents at Work Study surveyed more than 1,200 agency leaders and staff. 51% of frontline staff report burnout and stress. Burned-out staff are more than 2x as likely to look for a new job. (Attribution per search summary.) — [Insurance Business](https://insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/breaking-news/the-perfect-storm-of-stress--why-are-staff-at-insurance-agencies-burning-out-531589.aspx)
- 2025 PropertyCasualty360/NAPIA agent survey: the talent gap "persists" at organizations of every size, and "employees are aging out" — [PropertyCasualty360, 25 Nov 2025](https://www.propertycasualty360.com/amp/2025/11/25/2025-agent-survey-part-2-ai-fears-shrink-talent-gap-grows/)
- Insurance Journal's 2025 Agency Salary Survey: satisfaction with pay fell for the second year running — [Insurance Journal, Feb 2025](https://www.insurancejournal.com/magazines/mag-features/2025/02/24/812841.htm)
- MarshBerry put 2024 employee turnover at insurance brokerages at 16.4%, while Mercer put insurance/reinsurance at 8.2% (2024-25), the lowest of any US industry. Sources disagree, so the range is 8-16%. (The search summary did not tie these to an exact page. They were near the ITL and Sonant results.) — [Insurance Thought Leadership](https://www.insurancethoughtleadership.com/agent-broker/independent-insurance-agencies-face-staffing-crisis)
- A vendor page claims 25-35% annual CSR turnover at independent agencies. **It cites no source, so treat it as unreliable** — [Stealth Agents (vendor)](https://stealthagents.com/research/insurance-agency-staffing-costs-2026)
- 79% of Gen Z have never considered a career in insurance (Cake & Arrow 2025 study, cited in search summary) — [Sonant blog (vendor)](https://www.sonant.ai/blog/insurance-agency-employee-turnover)

### Inferences
- "Hard to hire" and "worth automating" point to different roles. MGMA ranks billers and coders low on hiring difficulty, yet front-office/admin roles have the highest turnover. In this lens, the cost to a practice is constant re-training and errors from churn, more than open vacancies.
- Burnout at insurance agencies (51%) plus an aging workforce suggests service work (endorsements, certificates, policy checking) is building up on fewer experienced account managers. That kind of task-level pain is what a monthly AI+human service could take on.

### Gaps
- BLS JOLTS and Occupational Outlook figures for bookkeeping clerks, customer service representatives, dispatchers, and billing clerks could not be pulled (bls.gov blocked, search budget used up).
- No 2025-26 data was gathered on: freight brokerage staff turnover (TIA), property-management leasing agent turnover (NAA/NARPM), AICPA pipeline figures, NFIB small-business job-opening reports, Indeed Hiring Lab, or LinkedIn Economic Graph.
- No clean CSR or account-manager turnover rate from a primary insurance survey was found.

## Key Question 2 — What do BPO companies say in 2025-2026 about what is being automated, what is growing, and what is still done by hand?

### Takeaway
All five large BPOs checked say the same thing. Simple voice/chat CX and Trust & Safety (content moderation) are shrinking because clients automate them. Money is moving to "AI services" (data labelling and model work), to platform-plus-people deals, and to outcome-based pricing. Genpact says outright that it is dropping "non-strategic" (low-value manual) work, at a cost of about 2 points of growth. The Philippines industry body cut its 2028 job target by 360k-650k workers. So the big firms are leaving smaller, messy, manual back-office work to someone else.

### Cited Findings
**Concentrix (FY2026, Q1-Q3)**
- Q3 FY26 (call ~29 Sep 2026): about half of revenue comes from work won in the last three years. Management splits it as ~$3B from clients with heavy transformation or AI-influenced revenue, $1.3B from **traditional services compressed by its iX Suite** platform, and ~$700M from newer high-value services (management claims) — [StockAlarm transcript page](https://pro.stockalarm.io/quote/CNXC/earnings-calls)
- Q3 FY26: net new-logo sales involving AI rose 63% sequentially — [MarketBeat Q3 summary](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/transcript-concentrix-q3-earnings-call-highlights-2026-09-29/)

- Q1 FY26: technology wins up more than 61% year on year. Signed annual contract value for solutions that include AI more than doubled sequentially — [Motley Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/06/02/concentrix-cnxc-q1-2026-earnings-transcript/). About 60 enterprise iX deals; iX ARR of at least $100M expected by year-end — [MarketBeat Q1](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/concentrix-q1-earnings-call-highlights-2026-03-24/)

EXL agreed to buy iMerit and became an OpenAI Services Partner — [EXL Q2 release](https://www.exlservice.com/about/newsroom/exl-reports-2026-second-quarter-results); [MarketBeat Q1](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/exlservice-q1-earnings-call-highlights-2026-04-29/)

**Philippines (IBPAP, revised roadmap July 2026)**
- 2028 targets cut from $59B / 2.5M FTE to a best case of $50.5B / 2.14M and a downside of $43.3B / 1.85M "AI-enabled workers". Reasons given: AI adoption and global competition — [Manila Bulletin](https://mb.com.ph/2026/07/14/ai-competition-prompt-it-bpm-industry-to-cut-2028-growth-targets); [Metrobank Wealth Insights](https://wealthinsights.metrobank.com.ph/news/ai-global-competition-force-philippines-it-bpm-industry-to-cut-targets)

### Inferences
- **Being automated at scale:** Trust & Safety/content moderation and tier-1 CX (TaskUs −12.3% T&S, Teleperformance T&S decline, Concentrix's "$1.3B traditional services compressed").
- **Growing:** AI data/annotation services, platform-plus-people deals, and outcome-based pricing. The large BPOs are selling "AI + humans" to enterprises, not to SMBs.
- **Still done by hand and being left behind:** Genpact's "non-strategic work" exit and Concentrix's "move offshore and cut spend" point to low-margin manual back-office work: verification calls, data entry from documents, payer and carrier follow-up. Mid-market and SMB buyers get this through VA marketplaces (see KQ3), not through large BPOs. That is the gap a small AI+human-in-the-loop services firm could fill.
- Philippine headcount is still growing (~+50k in 2026) while targets are cut. Manual offshore work is still rising for now, but growth expectations are falling.

### Gaps
- TTEC, WNS (now part of Capgemini, per prior knowledge — unverified), Firstsource (US healthcare collections), Startek, and iQor were not covered (budget).
- No Everest Group or ISG 2025-26 summaries were gathered.
- No BPO disclosure was found that breaks out healthcare RCM, insurance back office, or logistics task volumes.

## Key Question 3 — What do mid-market and SMB companies pay for offshore VA/BPO staff in 2025-2026, and for which tasks?

### Takeaway
Philippine task-specific staff (dental/medical billing, insurance verification) cost **$5-12/hour** on direct-hire marketplaces. Managed VA firms charge about **$700-1,600/month full-time**. Specialised CS or bookkeeping hires cost **$900-1,800/month** through placement firms. Offshore freight track-and-trace is quoted at **$15-20/hour**. Per-task outsourcing exists in dental verification at **$3-12.50 per verification**. These prices set the ceiling for an AI+human monthly service.

### Inferences
- A practical ceiling for an AI+HITL service: one PH task specialist costs $500-1,600/month all-in. A per-task service has to beat about $3-8 per unit (dental verification) or about $10-20/hour of replaced labour (freight).
- **Firms do not buy "a VA" in the abstract.** The job posts are task-specific (eligibility, AR follow-up, check calls). That means buyers can already describe the work as a unit, which makes per-unit pricing possible.

### Inferences
Test key: (a) at least weekly · (b) measurable money or customer loss · (c) already paid for · (d) the person who feels it can approve spend · (e) AI+human checker can do most of it in 2026 · (f) not solved by a tool under $100/month or a free bundle.

**P1 — Dental insurance eligibility and benefits breakdowns.** *Staff log into payer portals or call payers to get coverage %, frequencies, history, waiting periods and maximums, then retype them into the practice management system (Dentrix/Eaglesoft/Open Dental) the day before each appointment.*
- Industry/size: general and specialty dental, 1-20 locations and small DSOs. Feels it: front desk / insurance coordinator. Approves: owner dentist or office manager (DSO: RCM director).
- Frequency/hours (estimate): 20 insured patients/day × 12 min (CAQH manual) = 4 hrs/day ≈ **20 hrs/week per location**. This matches the vendor case studies (15-20 hrs/week).
- Dollar cost (estimate): ~400 verifications/month × $4.25-8.25 (outsourced price sheets) = **$1,700-3,300/month per location**. In-house at an assumed $25/hr fully loaded × 87 hrs/month ≈ $2,170/month (wage assumption unverified). PH VA at $5-6/hr × 87 hrs ≈ $435-520/month.
- Today: front desk staff, PH VAs, per-verification outsourcers (eAssist and others), AI software (Zuub, Overjet, Pearl, Dentalbase, Vyne Trellis).
- Crowding: **high**. Several AI vendors plus many offshore shops.
- 24-month outlook: volume is stable (the PPO mix keeps it daily), but AI pushes the price per verification toward $1-3. Practice management vendors may bundle it (prior knowledge, unverified).
- Reach buyers (prior knowledge, unverified): AADOM (office managers), Dentaltown, Dental Economics, study clubs, PMS integration marketplaces, DSO conferences.
- US count (prior knowledge, unverified): on the order of 120-140k dental practice locations.
- Against: already crowded with funded AI vendors and $4 human verifiers, and PMS vendors are well placed to bundle it.
- Tests: **6/6** (f passes: cheapest dedicated tool found is Zuub at $299/month; basic 270/271 eligibility in the PMS gives active/inactive status only, not full breakdowns — prior knowledge). Attractiveness **6/10**.

## Key Question 5 — Who approves the spend, and how do they buy today?

### Takeaway
In every SMB problem here, the person who feels the pain is one level below the person who signs: front desk → owner dentist or office manager; biller → practice administrator; T&T rep → broker owner or VP ops; CSR → agency principal. They buy offshore labour through VA marketplaces and managed VA firms on monthly plans ($699-1,800/month, cited in KQ3), and they buy per-task outsourcing on per-unit price sheets ($3-12.50 per dental verification, cited). They buy software through vertical system integrations.

### Cited Findings
- Managed VA firms sell month-to-month plans with a dedicated assistant plus a customer success manager. Pricing is often shown only on a demo — [Hackceleration](https://www.hackceleration.com/labs/wing-assistant-pricing); [PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/wingassistant)
- Dental per-task outsourcers sell per-location tiers plus a per-verification fee — [eAssist](https://dentalbilling.com/pricing-dental-insurance-verification/)
- Job posts for PH dental/medical VAs are placed by practices or staffing intermediaries and are task-specific — [Workable](https://jobs.workable.com/jobs/d6227c7a-beae-419d-927f-522cf653aebd.md)

### Inferences
- A services firm can copy the buying pattern buyers already use: a per-location monthly base plus per-unit pricing, sold through vertical associations and software integration marketplaces (channels listed per problem in KQ4, from prior knowledge).
