## 2. Key findings

1. Paid audit credited to the project is the most common "audit-first" shape I found. Examples: a $1,500 72-hour audit on Upwork, credited in full against a $4,000 to $8,000 rescue sprint; Rocking Tech (UK) 3-week discovery at GBP 4,500, credited in full against a later build; aibrify.com $100 diagnosis credited toward the fix. Seen across 3 different vendors and 3 price tiers.
   Grade: moderate (consistent across vendors) for "this shape exists"; weak for "this shape works", no conversion data.
   Type: vendor marketing.

Date: seen 2026-10-21 (page dates unknown, Rocking Tech blog post dated 12 May 2026).

## 6. What this means for the offer
- The common shape in this market is: cheap or free audit, then a quoted fixed-price fix. A paid audit that is credited in full against the sprint appears at three price points ($100, $1,500, GBP 4,500). If Wanile uses this shape, price the audit near $1,500 and credit it; that gives a filter against tyre-kickers in theory, but no conversion data supports this.
- Wanile's "$2,000 per week" sits below the Upwork 2-3 week sprint ($4,000 to $8,000) and well below "Lovable to production" quotes ($8,000 to $15,000). A weekly rate with cancel-any-week plus a last-week refund (Wavect) is the closest peer. Note these are asking prices from vendors, not what buyers paid.
- A "7 days" productized offer was not found in one search. That may be an open position or just a search miss. Needs re-check before claiming it is open.
