## 6. What this means for the offer
- Do not design the offer or the ad budget on the claim that rescue sellers profit from Meta ads. It is unproven here. Treat Meta as a test, not a proven channel.
- A free or low-priced audit (free to $500, about 2 to 5 days) is the common front door among sellers. A paid audit credited to the sprint is a way to differ from the free-audit crowd.
- The visible price band is wide (from $199 audits and $25-$99/hour firms up to $5,000+ projects). Position above the floor needs evidence from other tracks.
