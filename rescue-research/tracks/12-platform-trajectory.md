## 6. What this means for the offer
- The pool of vibe-coded apps is growing fast, not shrinking: Lovable alone adds about 1M projects a week, and Replit, Base44 and Cursor are all growing revenue at multiples. The top of the funnel for rescue is expanding for at least the next 12 months.
- The easiest-to-productise rescue items (RLS, secrets, dependency and config holes) are the exact items platforms are now scanning for. A security-only audit is the part most likely to be commoditised first. The offer should lead with working-product outcomes (payments, auth flows, data model, deploy, maintainability) and use the scan results as an input, not the deliverable.
- Lovable Cloud's "no one-click migration to own Supabase" and the 80% non-technical base create migration and handoff work that platforms do not do themselves. Position as "move you off the platform and harden" as well as "fix on the platform". Platform scanners are paid for by the user's own subscription, so expect clients to arrive with scan reports in hand.

## 7. What this means for the GO gates
- Gate 5 (platform improvements unlikely to erase demand within 12 months): UNCLEAR, LEANING SUPPORTS. See reasoned view below. Evidence is moderate on the volume side and thin on the "platforms offer an expert or done-for-you service" side because that search was cut off.
- Gate 1 (buyers paying $1,500+): unclear. This track found no buyer-payment evidence. The only signal is that only a minority of Lovable builders earn revenue (35% to about 10%, conflicting), which caps how many can pay $2,000.
- Gate 3 (repeatable work): weakly supports. Platform scanners list RLS, secrets, dependencies and DB config as standard categories, which suggests the common failure list is stable and checklist-able.

### Gate 5 reasoned view
Evidence that platforms WILL erase demand (shrink):
- Lovable has built-in scanners (dependencies, AI code review, RLS analysis, DB config) plus Wiz (May 2026).
- Lovable Cloud is on by default and bundles database, auth, storage and functions, which removes the hand-wired Supabase mistakes.
- Replit moved from pre-deploy Semgrep (May 2025) to a Security Agent (Apr 2026) to black-box pen tests with agent remediation (Aug 2026). Cadence is a new security layer every 3 to 4 months.
- Base44 gross margin rising from near zero to about 60% means Wix can afford more compute per user, which usually means better agents.
- Huge funding ($13.3B, $9B) means big engineering budgets aimed at reliability.
Evidence that demand WILL persist or grow (grow):
- Volume: 1M new Lovable projects per week; 50M total; Replit and Base44 growing revenue 1.5x to 2x within months. Even a 50% reduction in broken rate leaves more broken apps in 12 months than today.
- Mix: 80% of Lovable builders are non-technical (vendor claim), growing fastest in founders, designers and sales roles. They cannot judge scanner output or approve agent patches. Replit requires a human to approve fixes and republish.
- Scanners miss things: Replit says its black-box scan found an unauthenticated admin dashboard the code scanner missed. First-generation scanners find holes, they do not fix business logic, payment edge cases, data model errors or performance.
- Press framing: [PARAPHRASE, NOT VERBATIM] "Vibe Coding's Maintenance Test Still Looms" (TechTimes) and the Lovable "48 days of exposed projects" headline (TNW) show security and maintenance are unresolved even for the leader.
- Migration friction: no one-click move from Lovable Cloud to your own Supabase.
- Wiz integration is for teams already on Wiz, not for a solo founder.
- The feature curve has been 2 years of improvement while the rescue chatter (HN threads in the brief) kept rising.
Net read: within 12 months (to Oct 2027) platforms are likely to remove the cheapest, most standard faults (basic RLS, exposed secrets, auth wiring). They are unlikely to remove the demand for finishing, payments, data-model repair, performance, migration and a human accountable for production. Demand in total is likely to grow with the base. The risk is composition: the low-end audit slice shrinks and the market shifts toward larger, harder jobs. A second risk, not tested: a platform or Vercel-style partner launching a "done-for-you expert" tier. No evidence found either way (search cut off). Verdict on this gate: UNCLEAR LEANING PASS. Confidence: low to moderate. Close it with a follow-up search on expert programs, churn data and Replit Agent 3/4 feature detail.
