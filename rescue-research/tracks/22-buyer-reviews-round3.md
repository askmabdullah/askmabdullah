## 6. What this means for the offer
- The review platforms do not prove a $1,500+ rescue buyer yet. The three best reviews (Lovable to production at GeekyAnts, Bolt rebuild at Connectish, AI-code audit at Cleveroad) are the right shape of buyer. Their cost bands are unread. Someone with a browser must open the three Clutch pages and copy the "Project size" field before gate 1 can be called either way.
- The buyer who pays an agency is a founder with an investor-ready prototype (R1) or two products and an engineering mindset (R3), not the Fiverr crowd. R3 is the closest match to our first step: a written audit with file-level findings and a priority fix list, and "no pitch for a rewrite". That framing is a selling point to copy.
- Agencies that win these reviews list profile minimums of $5,000 to $25,000+. Our $2,000 sprint is below their entry point and far above Fiverr and Upwork buyer budgets ($20 to $1,250). The open gap is the middle: audit at about $1,500 (Soatech lists EUR 1,500) leading to a sprint.
