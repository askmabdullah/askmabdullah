## 7. What this means for the offer (3 bullets)
- Listing in the Lovable Expert directory and the Base44 partner marketplace is cheap and worth testing as a side channel, but nothing found shows these send paying rescue clients. Treat them as unproven. Do not build the ad budget logic on them.
- Base44's in-product hire marketplace shows listed rates of $10 to $100/hr, one at $180/hr (self-declared). A rescue sprint at about $2,000 per week must be priced against these listings plus Fiverr, so the offer needs a visible proof point (audit report, security fixes) to stand apart.
- The programs reward Lovable and Base44 portfolio work (shipped projects, traffic, builder levels). Wanile should ship a few Lovable and Base44 projects first, and use a small separate brand for the under-5-person Expert track.

## 8. What this means for the GO gates
- Gate 1 (buyers paying $1,500+): unclear. No directory shows what clients pay. Only self-declared hourly rates ($10 to $180/hr) from Base44 listings; no completed-job prices found.
- Gate 2 (channel cost per client under 30%): unclear. Directories are free to join (Lovable Expert is free), so cost per client could be very low, but lead volume is unknown. No agency reviews of lead quality found.
- Gate 3 (repeatable work): not touched.
- Gate 4 (position above the floor): weak support. Platforms certify by shipped projects and a badge, so a credible portfolio and certification could set Wanile above anonymous listings. Cheap Base44 partner rates show the floor.
- Gate 5 (platforms won't erase demand in 12 months): unclear. No in-house rescue or "done for you" service found, but that part was not fully searched. Replit's Hire App Builder page and Base44's in-product marketplace show platforms moving into matchmaking, which could capture the lead and take a cut (Base44 possibly 15%).
