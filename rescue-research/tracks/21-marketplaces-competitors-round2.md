## 6. What this means for the offer
- A $2,000 sprint has a real but thin buyer base on Upwork: two $2,000 budgets, both old, both finish/launch jobs with a backend task (Stripe/Twilio, QA/migration). Frame the first sprint around "finish the backend and launch" (payments, auth, RLS, deploy). That matches both $2,000 jobs and the sellers' repeat fix list.
- A cheaper entry product has real market anchors: audits at $49 to $499 and $150 to $750 cleanup tiers. A paid audit around $350 to $500 credited to the sprint matches Flow Lab ($350), Base44Devs ($497) and Arkroot (EUR 399), and filters out the $5 to $50 crowd.
- Do not price against Pakistan's visible floor ($17/hr, $40 to $100 gigs). Compete in the $1,400 to $2,500 band where small Western and EU studios already list prices, and win on proof the floor sellers lack: a named checklist, a written warranty, case studies, and security sign-off. GainCafe's "100% success guarantee" is vague, so a clear acceptance test is a way to stand out.

## 7. What this means for the GO gates
- Gate 1 (buyers paying $1,500+): unclear, leaning weak support. Two Upwork buyer budgets at $2,000 (2025-10-28, 2025-12-31) and a market of vendor prices at $1,400 to $10,000. No proof of payment, no named buyer, both jobs old. Round 1's "contradicts" should be softened to "unclear".
- Gate 4 (position above the low-cost floor): supports, moderate-weak. Floor is $5 to $50 on Fiverr (median $25) and $150 to $750 productized tiers on Upwork; Pakistani rivals found price at the floor. But a $2,000 sprint sits at the median of visible agency prices ($2,500), not above them, and at least 8 small vendors already sell in the $1,400 to $2,500 band. Position exists, it is not unique. Differentiation must come from proof and warranty.
- Gate 3 (repeatable work): supports, weak. Fiverr, Upwork and agency pages repeat the same list: RLS, auth, exposed keys, Stripe/webhooks, deploy, env vars, DNS.
- Gate 2 (channel cost under 30%): not touched. Upwork proposal counts were not visible, so cost per client on Upwork cannot be computed. Connects cost and win rate: no evidence found.
- Gate 5 (platform improvements): not touched. Base44 export in beta as of mid-2026 and Replit "fix loops" with non-refundable agent charges (as cited by agency posts) keep migration/rescue demand alive for now (vendor sources).
